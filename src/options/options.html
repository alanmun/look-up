Plain-English explanation optional

Everything above works with no account and no configuration. This section adds one thing the dictionary cannot do: restating a sentence in plain English. It is off until you turn it on, and until then the extension has no permission to reach any AI provider at all.

When enabled, the text you select is sent to the provider you connect. That is a real change in where your reading goes — everything else in Look Up only ever contacts Wiktionary.
